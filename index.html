FAQ

How long does it take?

Typically 7 days from approval to launch — a chatbot alone can be faster.

Do I need a website already?

No. The Chatbot + Website package includes a full new site. If you have one, we add the chatbot to it.

Will the chatbot make things up?

No. It's trained only on your real business information. When it doesn't know something, it says so and connects the visitor with your team.

Can it work in other languages?

Yes — the website and chatbot can be built in other languages on request.

What do you need from me?

Your services, prices, hours, and FAQs — plus a 20-minute call. We handle everything technical, including domain and hosting setup.

Are there really no monthly fees?

Correct. You pay once. The chatbot is built to run on minimal ongoing AI cost, which is bundled into your package.
